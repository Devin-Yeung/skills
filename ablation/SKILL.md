# Ablation

Apply Occam’s razor: a structure earns its cost only when evidence shows that its simpler alternative loses a needed capability.

1. Name the structure to ablate, its apparent cost, and the simpler candidate.
2. Choose evidence proportional to the blast radius and uncertainty.
3. Remove, inline, or merge the structure; migrate only behavior the evidence shows is needed.
4. Gather the chosen evidence. Keep the simpler design when it is supported; otherwise preserve or narrow the structure.
